Operational Root Cause: The Dinner Peak Surge

Severe Capacity Strain Between 19:00 and 22:00

Key Peak Hour Metrics (19:00 - 22:00)

  • Volume Surge: 31,500 orders (31.5% of total volume) placed in this 4-hour window.
  • Cancellation Spike: Peak cancellation reaches 14.8% (vs. 9.2% morning/afternoon).
  • Delay Premium: Average delay increases by +11.4 minutes during dinner rush.

Primary Delay Drivers

  • Active Rider Deficit: Active fleet availability remains flat while order volume surges 3.2x.
  • Kitchen Congestion: Biryani and Seafood prep times climb past 32 minutes during peak hours.
  • Delivery Distance: Orders traveling > 8 km suffer a 48.2% delay rate.
Speaker Notes (1.5 min): The peak hour surge is QuickBite's single greatest operational vulnerability. From 7 PM to 10 PM, order volume surges by over 300%, but active rider capacity remains stagnant. Riders get stuck in transit queues, restaurant counters overflow, cancellations surge to nearly 15%, and delivery delays compound exponentially.

Customer Sentiment & Retention Economics

Rating Elasticity and the High Cost of First-Order Churn

Delay vs. Customer Rating Elasticity

  • Early Arrivals (≤ 0 min): 4.21 ⭐ (68.4% 5-star reviews)
  • On-Time (1–5 min): 3.92 ⭐ (54.1% 5-star reviews)
  • Moderate Delay (16–30 min): 3.28 ⭐ (22.6% 5-star reviews)
  • Severe Delay (> 60 min): 2.48 ⭐ (54.8% 1-star reviews)
  • Conclusion: Every 15 min delay erodes ~0.35 stars.

Customer Retention Dynamics

  • One-Time Users: 13.5% of customers (2,694 users) order only once, contributing just 3.5% of total revenue.
  • Active Repeat Rate: 85.5% among active ordering customers (79.6% across all signups).
  • Loyal Customers (10+ orders): Generate an average lifetime spend of ₹3,730 (9.5x higher than 1-time users).
  • Churn Driver: 64% of churned 1-time customers suffered a delay > 20 min or cold food on their first order.
Speaker Notes (1.5 min): Customer ratings are exceptionally sensitive to delivery punctuality. Severe delays (>60 min) drop average ratings to 2.48 stars and generate 54.8% 1-star reviews. Most damagingly, when a first-time customer experiences a late delivery, over 60% never order from QuickBite again.
